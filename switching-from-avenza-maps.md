# Avenza Maps changed in 2026. How do I move my maps?

> In 2026 Avenza Maps' free plan stopped importing maps you bring yourself, its Plus plan is limited to 20 of your own maps for non-work use, and any work, academic, government or volunteer use needs Pro. Your PDF maps are ordinary files: get them back from where you downloaded them (or export them from Avenza), export your placemarks as KML or GPX if your plan allows, and open both in another geospatial PDF app.

## What changed

- **Free plan:** "Recreational only". You can use maps from Avenza's store, record tracks and add placemarks, but you can no longer import your own maps. Free imports were removed in March 2026.
- **Plus:** $34.99 a year per device in the US (CA$49.99, A$59.99), up to 20 of your own maps, for non-work use only.
- **Pro:** from $169.99 a year per device in the US. Avenza's listing says Pro is "required for commercial, academic, government, and professional use", and that includes volunteer groups.
- **Map store:** Avenza merged with Blue Marble Geographics in April 2026 and froze its map store to new publishers and map updates, which is why agencies such as the Forest Service and trail groups are telling people to find their maps elsewhere.

## Move your maps

Your maps are files: geospatial PDFs (often called GeoPDFs) or GeoTIFFs. Any app that reads those formats can open them.

1. **Get the files back.** The simplest way is to download them again from where they came from: a forest or park website, your GIS team, your crew lead's email. Avenza can also export a map you imported as a PDF or TIFF.
2. **Export your placemarks and tracks.** In Avenza, open the map's layers and export them as KML or GPX if your plan allows exporting. KML keeps names, descriptions and colours; GPX keeps tracks and waypoints.
3. **Open them in the new app.** On an iPhone, share the PDF to the app, or save it to Files and import it. Then open the KML or GPX file the same way.
4. **Check the position.** Open a map where you are and make sure your location dot lands where you're standing.

Keep Avenza installed until you've checked every map you need, then delete it.

## What to look for in a replacement

- **Work use allowed** on the plan you can afford, if you use maps for a job, a course or a volunteer group.
- **Opens geospatial PDFs and GeoTIFFs**, including multi-page atlases.
- **Works with no signal:** maps stored on the phone, GPS in airplane mode.
- **Exports** placemarks and tracks (KML, GPX, CSV) so you're never locked in again.
- **Sharing** with a crew or group if you hand out maps.
- **Bluetooth GNSS receivers** if you use one.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will I lose the maps I already have in Avenza?

No. Maps already in the app stay there, and the original files are still wherever you downloaded them from. Moving means opening the same files in another app.

### Can I export the maps I bought from the Avenza Map Store?

Maps bought from the store are licensed for use inside Avenza, so they can't be moved. Many were made by agencies that also offer them free on their own websites, such as the Forest Service, parks and trail groups.

### Do I need Avenza Pro for work?

Under Avenza's current terms, yes: Pro is required for commercial, academic, government and professional use. Other apps have their own terms; Mapsheet allows work use on every plan, including the free one.

### Which file formats should I keep?

Keep the original geospatial PDF or GeoTIFF of each map, plus a KML or GPX of your placemarks and tracks. Those formats open in nearly every map app and GIS program.
