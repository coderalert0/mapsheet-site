Fire information

Fire perimeters, incidents and hotspots in Mapsheet are copied from public agency feeds (NIFC/WFIGS, NASA FIRMS, Natural Resources Canada's CWFIS, the BC Wildfire Service, the NSW Rural Fire Service and the Queensland Fire Department) and can be hours old, incomplete, estimated or wrong. A satellite hotspot is a heat detection, not a confirmed fire, and a missing fire doesn't mean there is none. Fire information in Mapsheet is not a warning service and must never be used for evacuation, safety or tactical decisions: follow official warnings, your incident's briefings and the authorities in charge.

It is not a personnel accountability, tracking or emergency locator system; keep your organization's own check-ins and radio procedures. Share positions only with people who agree to it.
